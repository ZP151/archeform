Status: accepted. Actual consumer attempt 2 and the single independent ordinary
review pass, with P0/P1/P2 0/0/0. PM accepts Training Funding as the sixth local

## Product and reuse

Employees request funding for a named course, positive fee, session date and
justification. Managers return requests with a reason or approve them; finance
reads decisions. The same request supports correction and resubmission, preserving
versions, decisions and audit history. Approval records a funding decision only:
payment, reimbursement execution, enrollment, calendar delivery, entitlement and
private identity are outside this local scope and require clarification.

The production change is one first-party catalogue row under accepted ADR-0065.
It uses the existing Approval family and shared numeric capability delivered at
`9eabe817` under ADR-0069. No product-specific runtime branch, renderer, database
schema, package, component, image or icon is added. The positive fee uses an
explicit exclusive zero minimum; no currency unit or rounding policy is inferred.

The reuse search and unchanged UI assets are recorded in
[numeric capability acceptance](numeric-field-domains.md). This product composes
the approved cobalt workspace, photo fallback, Lucide icons, native controls,
numeric/date summaries and decision history. Existing Publication assertions are
retained while its generic E2E helper gains optional numeric summary and correction
parameters.
