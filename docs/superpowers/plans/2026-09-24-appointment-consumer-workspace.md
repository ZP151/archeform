## Product outcome

An ordinary customer selects a service and available time on a phone, requests
an appointment, returns to its saved details and cancels with a reason when
needed. Staff confirm, reschedule and inspect the same appointment/history on
desktop. Administrators manage services and offered schedules through visible
controls. Each role sees meaningful labels, valid actions and recoverable errors.

This replaces the defective consumer interface for one existing logical product.
It adds no logical product or runtime family to the coverage score. Historical
V1 remains reproducible; V2 is a fresh application revision, not a database
migration. Shared fixture roles are openly labelled local demo identities.
