# Market desk research

Use public, attributable evidence only. Prefer official product documentation, source repositories, release notes, pricing, public job listings, and engineering publications.

## Workflow

1. State the decision or hypothesis being investigated.
2. Collect direct sources and record URL, date when available, observed fact, and confidence.
3. Separate inference from fact and identify the product decision affected.
4. Update `docs/market-validation.md` only for durable, decision-relevant evidence.

Never ask the founder to recruit, interview, message, or purchase access from prospects.
