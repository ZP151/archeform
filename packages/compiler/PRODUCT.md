# Generated applications

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Ordinary users who describe a business need and expect a working application,
including people submitting requests and colleagues deciding them on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Reduce the user's application-development effort. Assemble useful, responsive
applications from reviewed business definitions, components and assets in minutes.
The founder has repeatedly rejected sparse generated interfaces as unfinished.

## Positioning

The Application Graph is the source of truth. Models select and configure
reviewed definitions; deterministic compilers assemble the published application.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve Draft, Publish and immutable Compilation. Current canonical definitions
are Restaurant, Expense and Purchase. Expense and Purchase share approval
behavior. Demo roles are local fixture controls, not production authentication.
Production identity, managed hosting and ordinary-user validation remain open.
UI text, source, tests and documentation are English. Preserve Graph custom copy,
fields, roles, permissions and theme tokens. Do not fabricate currency units,
people, business records, commercial claims or deployment readiness.

## Evidence on Hand

Business regression exists in the compiler and actual Purchase acceptance lane.
The B2 screenshots in `docs/acceptance/evidence/consumer-record-finding` were
rejected by the founder. Functional success does not establish visual acceptance.

## Product Principles

- Deliver the business outcome with few user decisions and no technical handoffs.
- Compose shared, tested assets before inventing another application template.
- Make the primary task and important record information immediately usable.
- Prove responsive behavior, real interactions and actual visual finish together.

## Open Decisions

No new brand constraint was supplied. The optional clarification remains pending;
implementation follows the founder's existing request for rapid substantive repair.
